# Financial Statement Review

1. Load statements from upload or connected Drive/SharePoint. Check that the balance sheet balances and cash flow ties to cash movement; report any break first.
2. Do common-size (% of revenue, % of total assets) and period-on-period analysis.
3. Review quality: revenue vs receivables growth, margin movement, working capital build, debt and interest burden, capex vs depreciation, contingent items.
4. Compute key ratios with formulas shown. Use Indian terms (Schedule III headings, crore/lakh).
5. Write commentary: headline, 3-5 key observations, red flags, questions for management.
6. Deliver as Word (PEREPA EDU navy/gold) for clients; keep working numbers in Excel.
